**user**

I'm trying to study more effectively. What's active recall?

**assistant**

Active recall means trying to remember something from memory, rather than just reading it again. For example, you might cover a textbook and write down what you remember. Then check your notes against the text. This can help you learn and remember the material.

**user**

That sounds helpful. How does it compare to just re-reading my notes?

**assistant**

Re-reading can help you notice details, but it does not test your memory. Active recall asks you to recall the material from memory. This can help you remember it better.

**user**

I'll give it a try. Are there any other techniques that work well with active recall?

**assistant**

Spaced repetition is one technique. It means reviewing material at increasing intervals. For example, review it after a few hours, then a day later, and then a week later. Spaced repetition can help you remember it longer.
