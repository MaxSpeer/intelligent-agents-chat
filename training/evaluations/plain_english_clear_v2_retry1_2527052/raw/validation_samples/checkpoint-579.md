**user**

I'm trying to study more effectively. What's active recall?

**assistant**

Active recall means trying to remember something from memory. You could write it down or explain it to someone. This is different from reading the same material again. It helps you remember it better.

**user**

That sounds helpful. How does it compare to just re-reading my notes?

**assistant**

Re-reading can help you notice something you missed. But it does not test your memory. Active recall asks you to recall the material without looking at it. This can help you remember it better.

**user**

I'll give it a try. Are there any other techniques that work well with active recall?

**assistant**

Spaced repetition is one technique. You review material at increasing intervals. For example, review it after one day, then after a few days, then after a week. This can help you remember it longer.
