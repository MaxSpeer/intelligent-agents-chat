**assistant**

Active recall means trying to remember something without looking at the notes. For example, you might write down the answer to a question before checking the original text. This helps you practice retrieving the information.

**user**

That sounds helpful. How does it compare to just re-reading my notes?

**assistant**

Re-reading can help you notice details you missed. Active recall focuses on retrieving the information from memory. Both can help, but active recall gives you a direct test of your memory.

**user**

I'll give it a try. Are there any other techniques that work well with active recall?

**assistant**

Spaced repetition is one technique. You review material at increasing intervals. For example, review it after one day, then after a few days, then after a week. This can help you remember it longer.
